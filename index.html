Ruinborne

Keep the job. Lose the run. A pastel roguelite dive through five acts.

Play free in your browser Install it like an app

What it is

Pick a job at the Threshold, dive through the ruin chamber by chamber, and take what you can. Relics, curses and life end with the run. Your levels, classes, skills, weapons and familiars stay.

Twin-stick fights

Move with one stick and aim with the other. Each job has a dash, a cape spin and its own signature Y-skill.

Six jobs, 18 weapons

Each weapon changes your basic attack and gives you its own four-tier skill line.

Progress that sticks

Job levels to 50, four class tiers per job, skill loadouts, familiars with talents, and Ruin Rank.

A different run every time

Choose your next chamber by its sigil, collect relics and sets, take on curses for rewards, and swap jobs at a crystal mid-run.

Endless, Oaths and more

Oaths make the game harder for bigger rewards. Endless loops the acts deeper and deeper. Daily and weekly dives, plus a Codex of everything you've met.

Plays anywhere

Touch, keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad. Runs in the browser, installs like an app, and saves on your device.

The five acts

Each act has its own stone, light, music and boss.

Act I

Lantern Ruin

Dusty-rose courts lit by lanterns. Boss: the Ash Warden.

Act II

Ash Vaults

Sealed vaults full of ambushes. Boss: the Vault Tyrant.

Act III

Pale Throne

Pale lilac halls around the seat of ruin. Boss: the Throne of Ruin.

Act IV

Drowned Sanctum

Sea-glass floors, deep-water fog and teal light. Boss: the Drowned Saint.

Act V

Starlit Spire

Indigo stone under gold starlight, up out of the sea. Boss: the Hollow Star.

The jobs

Six jobs, each with four class tiers and three weapons.

Wanderer

Balanced bolts and a long dash. Mix skills from every class you've unlocked.

Thrown Dagger · Blade Spin · Glaive

Arcanist

Chain lightning that jumps further as you rank up, and fireballs that scorch the ground.

Wildfire Core · Thorned Prism · Frost Orbit

Reaper

A scythe that works best in the middle of the swarm.

Scythe Arc · Vampire's Bite · Chain Sickle

Hunter

Thin, fast shots. Keep kiting and never stand still.

Hunting Arrow · Barbed Shot · Scattershot

Knight

A sword that throws its own edge: crescents that cut through the line.

Oathblade · Dawn Edge · Warhammer

Puppeteer

Fights through puppets on strings that dart out, strike and come home.

Marionette · Threads · Jester Box
