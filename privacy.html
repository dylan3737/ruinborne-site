Ruinborne Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Ruinborne is a single-player game made by Dylan Dyal. This policy explains what the game and its partners do with your information.

What the game stores

Ruinborne saves your progress on your own device: your jobs and levels, unlocked acts, settings, whether you bought the Supporter pack, and a short log of your recent runs. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

Ruinborne is free. On Android, an ad only plays when you choose a reward (rising again once per dive, or doubling a dive's Ash). These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob collects information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and app details, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time under Settings, Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, under Google, Ads.

Purchases

The Supporter pack is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details; the game only asks Google Play whether you own the pack.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • We collect no personal information ourselves, and run no analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We never sell your information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
