Ruinborne Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Ruinborne is a single-player game made by Dylan Dyal. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short answer: it collects nothing.

What the game stores

Ruinborne saves your progress on your own device: your jobs and levels, unlocked acts, settings, and a short log of your recent runs. This stays on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No collection of personal information, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No advertising.
  • No sharing or selling of data to anyone.

Purchases

Ruinborne is a paid app. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
